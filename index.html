Privacy Policy for Badminton Training Together

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Badminton Training Together ("the App") is developed and operated by Ding Digital Innovation Lab (DDIL), based in the Netherlands. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when players use the App, including its training program, progress features, Gear Finder, and AI Coach.

We aim to collect and process only the information necessary to provide the App's features. We do not sell personal information and we do not use player information for advertising.

1. Information We Process

The App may process the following categories of information:

  • training progress, completed exercises and journey progress;
  • personal-best results, badges and achievements;
  • basic player preferences, such as the selected player/avatar;
  • Gear Finder answers and recommendations;
  • AI Coach usage and available AI Check credits;
  • videos voluntarily recorded or selected by a player for AI Coach analysis;
  • AI Coach analysis results and feedback; and
  • technical information necessary to operate the App and its backend services.

Some training and preference information may be stored locally on the user's device. Features that require backend processing, including AI Coach, may store information in private backend storage associated with the user's guest account.

2. Guest Accounts

The App does not require players to create a traditional account with a name, email address or password. A guest account may be created automatically to support features such as AI Coach and private backend data.

Backend access controls are designed so that a guest user can access only data and video files associated with that user's own account.

3. AI Coach Videos

AI Coach is an optional feature. A player chooses whether to record or select a badminton training video for analysis.

When AI Coach is used, the selected video is uploaded to private backend storage and processed to generate badminton technique feedback.

Successfully processed AI Coach videos may be retained privately for up to 30 days and are scheduled for deletion thereafter. Videos that fail processing or the App's video-quality checks are not intended to be retained as successful analyses.

Users should not upload videos containing unnecessary personal information, other people who have not agreed to be recorded, or content unrelated to badminton training.

4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

AI Coach uses AI infrastructure provided through Lovable and third-party AI model providers, including Google Gemini, to analyze submitted badminton training videos and generate feedback.

Information necessary to perform an AI Coach analysis may therefore be transmitted to these service providers for processing.

Badminton Training Together does not sell AI Coach videos, use them for advertising, or use players' training videos to train its own AI models. Third-party service providers process information according to their applicable service terms, privacy commitments and data-processing arrangements.

5. AI Coach Results and AI Checks

The App may store AI Coach results and information related to AI Check usage in the backend so that the service can operate correctly and prevent incorrect or duplicate use of AI Checks.

The App may offer free or purchased AI Checks. Information required to maintain an AI Check balance may be associated with the user's guest account.

6. Children and Young Players

Badminton Training Together is designed for junior badminton players. We take the privacy of children and young people seriously.

The App does not require a child to provide a name, email address, home address or other traditional profile information in order to use the training program.

Before a player uses AI Coach video upload for the first time, the App presents a parent/guardian consent notice. Parents and guardians should supervise the use of AI Coach by younger players and ensure that only appropriate badminton training videos are uploaded.

We do not knowingly use children's personal information for advertising, behavioral profiling or sale to third parties.

7. Advertising and Tracking

The App does not currently contain third-party advertising. We do not use player videos or training information for targeted advertising.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the App. These may include cloud infrastructure, storage and AI-processing providers such as Lovable and Google Gemini.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the security and integrity of the App and its users.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. AI Coach videos are stored in private storage, and backend access rules are designed to prevent users from accessing another player's private videos or results.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Different types of information may have different retention periods. AI Coach videos that are successfully processed may be retained for up to 30 days and are scheduled for deletion thereafter.

Training progress, AI Coach results, guest-account information and related service data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain user progress, protect the service, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

11. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

The current version of the App does not provide an in-app button for manually deleting individual AI Coach videos or analysis results.

A user, parent or guardian may contact us to ask a privacy question or request deletion of applicable personal data associated with the App.

Because the App uses guest accounts rather than traditional named accounts, we may need information that allows us to identify the relevant guest account before we can process a deletion request.

12. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on services including:

  • Lovable infrastructure and AI services;
  • Google Gemini for AI-assisted video analysis; and
  • cloud/database/storage infrastructure used by the App backend.

These providers may process information only as necessary for the services used by the App and according to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

13. International Data Processing

Some service providers may process information outside the user's country. Where applicable, service providers use contractual or other safeguards for international data transfers in accordance with applicable data-protection law.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services or applicable legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
