Badminton Training Together — Data Deletion Request

Badminton Training Together is developed by Ding Digital Innovation Lab (DDIL).

Badminton Training Together does not require users to create a traditional account with an email address and password. Some features use an automatically created guest account to store private app data.

How to request deletion

Step 1: Send an email to ningding0610@gmail.com.

Step 2: Use the subject: Badminton Training Together — Data Deletion Request.

Step 3: Include enough information for us to identify the relevant guest account or app data. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Because the App uses guest accounts rather than traditional named accounts, we may need to contact you for additional information that allows us to identify the relevant guest account before deletion can be completed.

Data that may be deleted

Depending on the features you have used, a deletion request may cover:

  • AI Coach videos associated with the relevant guest account;
  • AI Coach analysis results and feedback;
  • guest-account backend data;
  • AI Check usage and balance information associated with that guest account; and
  • other personal data stored by the backend and associated with the identified user.

Some training progress and preferences may be stored only on the user's device. Local app data can generally be removed by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

Video retention

Successfully processed AI Coach videos may be retained privately for up to 30 days and are scheduled for deletion thereafter.

Videos that fail processing or video-quality checks are not intended to be retained as successful analyses.

Data that may need to be retained

Some limited records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, dispute resolution, or compliance with applicable legal obligations.

Where retention is required, the information will be kept only for the period reasonably necessary for that purpose and will not be used for advertising.
